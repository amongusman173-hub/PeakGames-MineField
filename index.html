HOW TO PLAY

THE BASICS
  • Left Click Reveal a tile
  • Right Click Flag a suspected mine
  • Numbers show how many mines are in the 8 surrounding tiles
  • Reveal every safe tile to clear the floor
TRY IT — MINI MINEFIELD

Left click to reveal · Right click (or FLAG button) to flag · Clear all safe tiles to win

ROGUELIKE RULES
  • You have 3 lives ❤️ — each mine hit costs one
  • Lose all lives = permadeath, run ends
  • After each floor visit the 🛒 SHOP to buy perks & upgrades
  • Every 3rd floor is a ⚠ BOSS FLOOR with special rules
  • Earn 💎 gems to spend in the Gacha Machine between runs
  • Earn ⚡ EXP to unlock permanent skills in the Skill Tree
