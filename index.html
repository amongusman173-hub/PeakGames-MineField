HOW TO PLAY

  • Left Click Reveal a tile
  • Right Click Flag a mine — scored at floor end
  • Numbers show adjacent mine count
  • Reveal all safe tiles to clear the floor
  • You have 3 lives — each mine hit costs one
  • Lose all lives = permadeath
  • After each floor, visit the SHOP
  • Watch for floor modifiers — they change the rules!
  • Earn 💎 gems from runs to spend in the Gacha Machine
